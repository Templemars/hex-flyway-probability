# Kranstauber et al. 2015, global aerial flyways allowing efficient travelling

## Citation

Kranstauber, B. et al. (2015). *Global aerial flyways allow efficient travelling*. Ecology Letters.

## Why this paper matters for the project

This paper appears highly relevant because it is directly about global aerial flyways and efficient travel. That makes it potentially important both for the biological framing of our project and for the methodological idea that flyways may emerge from environmental movement efficiency.

## Why it belongs in `refs/methods/`

I am placing it in `methods` for now because the title and first read strongly suggest a close connection to efficient movement and flyway formation. Depending on a deeper reading, it may later prove to be a bridge between methods and background.

## Main relevance from first read

The paper appears to argue that:
- global aerial flyways can be understood in terms of efficient travel
- large-scale movement pathways may emerge from atmospheric structure and movement optimization

That is highly relevant to our project because our own model also seeks to explain flyways as structured outcomes of environmental conditions and movement trade-offs.

## Why this is useful for our sequel

This paper may help us with:
- framing flyways as efficiency-driven structures rather than only descriptive route patterns
- thinking about how much of flyway organization can be explained from atmospheric conditions
- connecting our graph-based route model to larger-scale flyway theory

## Relationship to the current project

This paper is likely important because it sits close to the conceptual core of the flyway problem itself, not only to a particular implementation.

It may help us think about:
- how to justify the existence of preferred migratory corridors
- how environmental structure shapes global route geometry
- how to discuss efficiency, optimization, and flyway emergence in the manuscript
